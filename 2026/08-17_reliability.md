# Towards a Science of AI Agent Reliability

* Authors: Princeton
* Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16666
* Date: 2026-06-02

## Notes

* reliability gains lag behind accuracy improvements
* single metric like mean task success rates is not sufficient
* in safety critical engineering domains, reliability is understood as a multi-dimensional property
* Consistency: Does the system behave the same way when run multiple times under identical conditions, or are outcomes
  highly variable?
* Robustness: When operating conditions deviate from nominal, does the system degrade gracefully or fail abruptly?
* Predictability: Can the system recognize when it is likely to fail?
* Safety: When failures occur, how severe are the resulting consequences?
* consistency: output, trajectory (distributional vs sequence), resource
* robustness: fault, environment, prompt
* predictability: calibration, discrimination, Brier score
* safety: compliance, harm severity
